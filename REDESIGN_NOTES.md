# Redesign Notes — Light Landing (light-landing-redesign branch)

The homepage has been rebuilt as a single-page light-themed landing modeled on clairvo.io. All existing pages (About, Use Cases, Pricing, Sales OS, ROI Calculator, Privacy, Terms, Cookies) are preserved at their original URLs and also accessible at `/archive/*` routes, which carry a `noindex` directive so only the new homepage is indexed. The new page lives at `/` and uses `background: #FAFAF7` with body text `#0D0B09` and the existing green accent (`#10B981`/`#065F46`). All copy has been generalized to B2B sales language — no mention of debt settlement, hardship, or vertical-specific terms. Section flow mirrors clairvo.io: minimal sticky nav → hero with three animated counters → problem cards (before/after) → four numbered product pillars with mock UI panels → features grid (6 cards) → results counters → dark final CTA → footer. Copy lives in `src/content/landing.ts` and stats in `src/lib/stats.ts` for easy updates without touching JSX. The announcement banner and old dark-theme components are untouched and continue to serve archived pages.
